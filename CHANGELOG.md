# Jingle Code Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Garland in the gaps between panels and above the status bar: twinkle, chase, color flow and warm white.
- Icicles under the main toolbar and under the active editor tab.
- Fireplace with a stocking in the status bar.
- Snow over the editor and a village along its bottom edge in four styles, with evening lights and residents.
- Snowdrift with a snowman at the bottom of the Project panel; a gift for every unpushed commit.
- Railway at the bottom of the bottom tool windows.
- Santa's yard at the bottom of the right tool windows, with letters for notifications.
- Snow caps on popups and context menus.
- Reactions to runs, tests and builds: a golden garland and fireworks on success, falling icicles on failure.
- Season: switches on December 1 and puts itself away after January 14.
- Settings | Appearance | Jingle Code with Minimal, Normal and Maximum modes.
